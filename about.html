Our Mission

Fresh Balance began in 2024 with a desire to bring people food that feels good in every way—fresh, nourishing, and thoughtfully made. By focusing on locally sourced ingredients and meals prepared from scratch, we set out to create a place where healthy eating is both satisfying and approachable.

Our Farmers

We partner with Ohio growers who share our commitment to fresh, wholesome ingredients. From leafy greens and vine-ripened tomatoes to orchard fruit and fragrant herbs, our local farm partners help us create meals that are both nourishing and flavorful.
